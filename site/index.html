Farborn

You wake up at a campfire with no memories, take a job for some quick coin, and steal the wrong thing: the power of a god whose kingdom fell 500 years ago.

Play on Roblox · coming later Learn more

The game

Aeloria is a world of six nations, each protected by a god. Above them drift the broken islands of a seventh kingdom that fell long ago. Travel from nation to nation, meet each god and find out who you really are.

Explore six nations

Climb, glide and swim across open regions. Every nation plays by its own rules, from festival islands to night-market canyons.

Fight as a party

Lead up to four heroes and swap between them instantly, mid-combo, to combine their powers.

Seven elements

Mix elements into reactions: melt, freeze, overload, sweep and more. Smart combos beat raw power.

Grow your team

Meet new heroes through the story and wishes. Odds and pity are always shown in the game.

Play your way

Choose to play as a boy or a girl. Explore solo or with friends in co-op.

Made for everyone

Built for kids and teens: colourful, stylised fights with no gore and no real-world religion.

Under the hood

Every system in Farborn is designed and built in-house. The code and the full technical write-ups are public on GitHub.

Built

Elemental reactions

Statuses that react: nine reactions, from Scald and Lock to Starburst.

 Built

Damage model

Tunable defence, resistance curves with no immunity, crits and reaction damage.

 Built

Wish system

Soft and hard pity, a featured 50/50 with a guarantee, published odds and regional rules.

 Built

Player data

Saves with retries, automatic upgrades for old saves and safe shutdowns.

 Built

Combat

Instant feel on your screen, every hit confirmed by the server.

 Built

Party and movement

Swap between four heroes mid-fight for Relays, Covers and Pile-Ons; sprint, climb and glide.

 Built

Enemy AI

Telegraphed attacks you can dodge or Lift, enemies that take turns, elites you can Rattle.

 Designed

World and co-op

Streaming regions, a world that grows with you and four-player co-op.
