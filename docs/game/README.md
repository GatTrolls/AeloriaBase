## The pitch

You wake up at a campfire with no memories, take a job for some quick coin, and steal the wrong
thing: the power of a god whose kingdom fell 500 years ago. Now it's inside you, and the only
people who might know who you are are the gods themselves.

## The world

Aeloria is a world of six nations, each protected by a god and each with its own element,
culture and twist on how it plays. Above the middle of the map drift the broken islands of a
seventh kingdom that fell five hundred years ago, visible from almost everywhere. Your journey
takes you from nation to nation to meet each god and piece together who you are.

## Pillars

1. **A mystery you uncover one god at a time.** Every region ends with a piece of your past.
2. **Every nation feels different.** Each one changes the rules: festivals and gliding,
   night markets, endless night and more.
3. **Friends first.** A found family of heroes, with side stories that can be funny, emotional
   or full of fighting.
4. **Villains with reasons.** Nobody in Aeloria is evil just for fun.

## How it plays

| Feature | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Main character | You choose to play as a boy or a girl |
| Exploration | Climb, glide and swim across open regions with puzzles, chests and hidden guardians |
| Combat | Real-time action: attack chains, an elemental skill and a big burst per hero |
| Party | Up to four heroes, swapped instantly mid-fight |
| Elements | Seven elements that combine into reactions (see [Elemental reactions](../systems/elemental-reactions.md)) |
| Heroes | A growing cast, unlocked through the story and wishes |
| Wishes | Banners with published odds and a visible pity counter (see [Wish system](../systems/wish-system.md)) |
| Progression | Hero levels, ascension, talents and gear |
| Home | A camp that grows into your home base |
| Co-op | Explore and fight with friends |

## What it is not

- No real-world religion. The gods are powerful beings from beyond the sky, more like legendary
  guardians than objects of worship.
- No blood or gore. Fights are stylised and colourful.
- No copied characters, names or art from other games. Farborn is inspired by the genre, not
  built from anyone else's work.

Character names, nation names and story details are announced in the [dev log](../devlog/README.md)
as they are finalised.
