## Short description (about 50 words)

Farborn is an anime open-world action RPG on Roblox. Wake up with no memories, steal the power
of a fallen god by accident, and travel six nations to meet the gods who might know who you
are. Build a party of four, swap mid-fight and chain elements into reactions.

## Long description (about 120 words)

You wake up at a campfire with no memories, take a job for some quick coin, and steal the wrong
thing: the power of a god whose kingdom fell 500 years ago. Now it's inside you, and the only
people who might know who you are are the gods themselves.

Farborn is an open-world action RPG made for Roblox by Owl On The Moon Studios. Explore six
nations that each play by their own rules, climb and glide across the world, and fight in fast
real-time combat with a party of up to four heroes. Swap heroes mid-fight to combine seven
elements into reactions, collect new heroes, and team up with friends in co-op.
