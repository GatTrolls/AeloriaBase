# The first combat systems are in

*2026-10-20 · M0 Foundation · Owl On The Moon Studios*

## What's new

Two of the systems at the heart of Farborn's combat now exist as real, tested code.

**Elemental reactions.** Hits leave an elemental aura on enemies that fades over a few seconds.
Hit them with a different element while it is there and you get a reaction: Steam and Melt
double your hit, Freeze locks enemies in place, Sweep spreads an element to everyone nearby,
and more. There are nine reactions across seven elements.

**Damage model.** Levels, defense, resistances, crits and reactions all feed one formula.
Nothing is ever fully immune to an element, and reaction damage scales with your level, so
every hero can make combos worth it.

## Behind the scenes

Both systems are pure Luau with no Roblox dependencies, so they run in automatic tests on every
change. There are now 24 tests across wishes, reactions and damage.

## What's next

Movement, the first hero's moveset and the first enemy, so all of this can be felt in a fight.
