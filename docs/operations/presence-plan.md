# Presence and operations plan

Everything here has a free option. Paid upgrades are listed separately and are optional.
Accounts are created by the project owner; nothing is posted publicly until the owner says so.

## 1. Identity first (M0)

| Item | Free option | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name check | Roblox search, USPTO and WIPO trademark search | Confirm "Aeloria" is not already a game or mark in use |
| One handle everywhere | e.g. `PlayAeloria` | Reserve on Roblox, X, TikTok, YouTube, Discord vanity later |
| Project email | A dedicated free Gmail or Proton address | Used for every account below, never a personal email |
| Logo and key art | Placeholder wordmark now, artist-made logo at M3 | Needed for icon, thumbnails, socials, site |
| Roblox group | Owns the experience, assets and dev products | Creating a group costs 100 Robux. Free path: start under a personal account and transfer later |

## 2. Planning and tracking

| Tool | Use | Audience |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GitHub Issues + Projects | Real backlog, sprints, bugs, linked to PRs | Team |
| GitHub milestones | M0 to M5 from ROADMAP.md | Team |
| Trello (free) | Public roadmap: Planned, In progress, Testing, Released | Players |

The team works in GitHub. Trello is a read-only window for players, updated at each milestone,
since many Roblox players expect a Trello link.

## 3. Website

- Hosted free on GitHub Pages from `site/` (address: `gattrolls.github.io/AeloriaBase`).
- Pages: home (pitch, trailer, Play on Roblox button), news and dev log, characters (only once
  announced), roadmap link, press kit, community links, privacy note.
- No cookies, trackers or sign-up forms, because the audience includes children.
- Optional later: custom domain (about 10 to 15 USD a year).

## 4. Dev log

- Source of truth: `docs/devlog/` in this repo.
- Cadence: a short progress clip weekly, a written post monthly or per milestone.
- Each post goes to the website, Discord #announcements, the Roblox group, X and a YouTube
  Short or TikTok.

## 5. Discord (open in M1, small)

- Free server, 13+ (Discord's minimum age).
- Channels: #rules, #announcements, #devlog, #roadmap, #general, #fan-art, #suggestions (forum),
  #bug-reports (forum), #playtest (role-gated), #team (private).
- Built-in AutoMod, Onboarding and Rules Screening on from day one. Two trusted moderators
  before inviting the public.
- Link from the Roblox experience through Social Links only.

## 6. Socials

| Platform | Why | Start |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Roblox group | Players already there; announcements and shouts | M0 |
| YouTube | Dev log videos, Shorts, trailers | M1 |
| TikTok | Short combat and VFX clips find young players fastest | M1 |
| X | Roblox dev community, other developers | M1 |
| Reddit, Bluesky, Instagram | Optional mirrors | M3+ |

Reserve handles now, post when there is something to show (the first good combat clip).

## 7. Wiki

- Until beta: this repo's docs are the internal wiki.
- At open beta: a player wiki on wiki.gg (free, light ads, apply for a wiki) or Fandom (free,
  heavier ads). Seed it with official pages; recruit editors from Discord.

## 8. Playtesting and feedback

- Private test place, invite testers by role in Discord.
- Bug reports: Discord forum, triaged into GitHub issues with the bug template.
- Roblox Creator Analytics for retention, session length and funnel; review weekly from M4.

## 9. Team and contributors

- Recruit on Roblox Talent Hub and the Roblox DevForum. Be clear about pay: unpaid, revenue
  share or commission.
- Any revenue share needs a short written agreement, including who owns the work. This repo is
  public, so add contributor terms before outside people contribute content.

## 10. Launch kit (M4 to M5)

- Experience icon, thumbnails and a 30 second trailer.
- Press kit page: fact sheet, logo, screenshots, contact email.
- Creator program: promo codes for YouTubers and TikTokers.
- Roblox localization (automatic translation is free), plus manual checks for key languages.
- Live-ops calendar: banners, events and patch notes on a fixed rhythm.
